# Product Direction

## One-liner

Distraction Save is not a blocker. It is a calm transition room that helps people recover attention and choose on purpose.

## Core idea

When a user opens a high-drift surface like the YouTube home feed, do not immediately ask for willpower.

Do this instead:

1. Lower arousal first.
2. Restore agency second.
3. Offer a clean next action third.

## Experience pillars

### 1. Freedom-first

- Do not shame.
- Do not trap.
- Do not force a confession.
- Keep the final choice with the user.

### 2. Calm-first

- Show nature, spaciousness, slow visual rhythm.
- Use short copy that sounds permissive, not moralizing.
- Prioritize recovery over friction.

### 3. Choice-over-drift

- Keep search and direct intent easy.
- Slow down infinite recommendation paths.
- Reduce passive consumption, not purposeful use.

## Signature copy

- 자동으로 끌려가지 않아도 됩니다.
- 당신의 시간은 아직 당신 것입니다.
- 천천히, 원하는 방향으로 가면 됩니다.

## Product ladder

### Chrome extension MVP

- YouTube home becomes a nature gateway.
- Search stays available.
- Watch page keeps the video, but collapses related videos and comments by default.
- 1-minute recovery mode gives points and a calm full-screen pause.

### Railway web app

Could add:

- Account and sync
- Daily task / today intention
- Nature scene library
- Mood or drift patterns by time of day
- Recovery history and gentle analytics
- Personal copy variations

### Mobile / desktop app

Future direction:

- Intercept distracting app launches with a calm room
- 30-second, 1-minute, and 3-minute recovery paths
- Saved “return targets” for work, study, exercise, sleep

## Architecture sketch

### Frontend

- Calm gateway screen
- Recovery screen
- Stats / reflection screen

### Backend

- User profile
- Today task
- Scene preferences
- Recovery sessions
- Point / streak ledger

### Content system

- Nature image packs
- Short muted ambient loops
- Copy sets by tone:
  - gentle
  - grounding
  - assertive

## Design notes

- Beautiful nature is allowed.
- Beauty is not the problem if the experience is bounded and calming.
- The danger is endless novelty, not visual comfort itself.

So the design target is:

- rich enough to soothe
- simple enough not to become another feed
